Finding a title

  1. Open TMDB Search (new tab) and search for the movie or show you want.
  2. Check the title and release year so you choose the right result.
  3. Choose a source and Movie or TV show above, then copy the title's numeric TMDB ID into the form. Use the ID, not its name or an IMDb ID.
  4. For TV shows, choose a season and episode. Select Load player, then press Play. If a title will not play, choose another source and load it again.
  5. Anime-only sources use AniList or MyAnimeList IDs instead. Check the ID label above, find the anime on that site, and enter its numeric ID and episode. These sources start with subtitled audio where an audio option is required.
  6. Availability and player controls vary by source. Alternate domains are listed separately. Entries marked “website only” cannot load a player here; use their Source website link.
