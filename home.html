Going back to the 404 page

Once a page is saved, the site opens straight to it. To get back to the plain 404 screen (so you can pick a different page, or so nobody looking over your shoulder sees anything), the cookie has to go.

If a hub gets stuck loading or shows an old version after its files changed, this throws away everything the game loader has cached (saved games are kept):

Or clear the cookie in Google Chrome by hand:

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Mac: ⌘+Shift+Delete). The "Delete browsing data" window opens.
  2. Set the time range to All time.
  3. Tick Cookies and other site data. You can untick everything else.
  4. Click Delete data, then reload this site. You should see "404 Not Found" again.

To delete the cookie for this site only, instead of every site: click the icon left of the address bar, choose Cookies and site data, then Manage on-device site data, and hit the trash can next to this domain.

Note: on a managed school or work Laptop the clear-data screen is sometimes locked by policy. The buttons above still work.
